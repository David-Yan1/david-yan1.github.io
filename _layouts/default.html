I am a CS PhD student at Princeton, advised by Jia Deng. My research is graciously supported by the Francis Robbins Upton Fellowship. I received my B.S.E. from Princeton, where I studied computer science, math, and philosophy.

I am interested in understanding what makes good data for computer vision, robotics, and representation learning. Recently, I have been thinking about how to develop synthetic data recipes that can surpass real-world data.
